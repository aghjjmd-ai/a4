About

A small site about small bags.

Pouchly began with a simple frustration: most bag advice is about large totes, while the bags people carry every evening, and on every trip, get a paragraph at most.

We write in detail about pouches, clutches, wristlets and mini bags. Our guides cover size, shape, craftsmanship and care, and our tools help you match a bag to what you carry and where you are going.

Independent by design

Pouchly does not sell bags and is not run by any brand. We do not accept payment for coverage. When we describe a feature or a material, it is because it helps you choose well.

How we write

Every guide is researched, written in plain language and checked by a second editor. We update guides when we learn something new and note the date on each one.
